# Second Year Students Survey

## Section C – Personal Focus (Questions 13–22)

---

### Question 13:

**How decided are you about what you want to become after Intermediate?**

### Most Common Response

* Still Exploring / Not Yet Decided

### Very Few Responses

* Mostly Decided
* Completely Decided

### Additional Feedback

Many students expressed confusion regarding career selection and uncertainty about future plans.

### Observation

Despite being in their final year of Intermediate, many students are still uncertain about their future careers and require structured career guidance.

---

### Question 14:

**Who has influenced your career decisions the most?**

### Responses

1. Parents
2. Friends
3. Teachers
4. Self (Very Few)

### Observation

Family remains the strongest influence on career decisions, while independent career decision-making is relatively uncommon.

---

### Question 15:

**If you could restart Intermediate, what would you do differently?**

### Given Options

* Study More Consistently
* Learn More Skills
* Explore Career Options Earlier
* Participate in More Activities

### Actual Responses

Most students selected "Other" and shared personal experiences such as:

* Family-related challenges
* Relationship distractions
* Personal issues affecting academics
* Emotional and social challenges

### Observation

Students reflected more on personal life experiences than academic decisions, indicating that emotional and personal factors significantly influence academic performance.

### Survey Insight

This suggests that students at this age may still be developing maturity and decision-making abilities, making mentorship and counseling highly valuable.

---

### Question 16:

**What is your biggest concern after completing Intermediate?**

### Most Common Responses

1. Entrance Examinations
2. College Admissions

### Other Responses

* Career Selection
* Financial Constraints

### Observation

Students are highly concerned about successfully transitioning into higher education and securing admissions into suitable institutions.

---

### Question 17:

**What advice would you give to First-Year students?**

### Most Common Response

* Focus on Academics

### Additional Advice

Students frequently mentioned:

* Avoid distractions
* Stay focused on studies
* Do not allow personal relationships to affect academics
* Prepare seriously for examinations
* Learn from mistakes made during Intermediate

### Observation

Many students shared lessons learned from personal experiences and encouraged juniors to maintain focus throughout their studies.

---

### Question 18:

**If your younger sibling asked for career advice, what would you suggest?**

### Most Common Response

* Follow Their Own Interests

### Other Responses

* Engineering & Technology
* Medical & Healthcare
* Business & Commerce

### Observation

Students generally believe individuals should pursue careers based on personal interests rather than external pressure.

---

### Question 19:

**What has been the most valuable lesson you learned during Intermediate?**

### Responses

* Hard Work
* Discipline
* Self-Learning
* Decision Making

### Additional Feedback

Students emphasized:

* Avoiding excessive social media usage
* Maintaining focus on goals
* Managing time effectively
* Avoiding negative distractions

### Observation

Many students recognized the importance of self-discipline and responsible use of technology.

---

### Question 20:

**Where do you see yourself five years from now?**

### Most Common Response

* Working in Professional Jobs

### Other Responses

* Preparing for Government Services

### Very Few Responses

* Running a Business
* Pursuing Higher Studies

### Observation

Most students aim to achieve employment and financial stability as early as possible.

### Other Responses

* Personal Interest

External guidance continues to influence career decisions more strongly than personal interest alone.
